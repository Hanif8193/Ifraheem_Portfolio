About

Design with personality.

I’m Muhammad Ifraheem, a graphic designer from Karachi who has spent 7+ years turning creative briefs into brands people remember.

I started as a junior designer in 2019 and grew into a senior role at Web Worx Labs, a Canada-based digital agency. Along the way I’ve worked with real estate, restaurants, education, e-commerce, petroleum, automotive, safety, technology, healthcare, skincare and Islamic banking brands.

My approach is simple: understand the brand, find the one idea worth shouting about, then keep every touchpoint consistent. I pair the Adobe Creative Suite and Figma with AI-assisted tools for faster exploration, while the taste and final decisions stay human.
